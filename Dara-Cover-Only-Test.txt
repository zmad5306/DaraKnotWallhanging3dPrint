  Quality > Precision > Arc fitting: OFF
  Quality > Precision > Resolution: 0.001 mm (previously 0.012 mm)
Resolution is the toolpath simplification tolerance, not layer height.
The saved native slice has no model-extrusion G2/G3 arcs. Startup and travel
routines can still contain arc commands.

WHY THIS TEST
The returned cover had a transverse scar in both physical orientations, with
a visible line in Preview. Inspection found a single watertight cover mesh,
and the existing G-code has rows of direction changes where fitted arcs meet.
That is a specific suspect, not a confirmed diagnosis of the physical scar.
Disabling arcs at the old tolerance produced coarser segments, so this test
also refines the tolerance. Digital checks confirm the intended changes;
they do not establish the printed finish or the cause of the rough edge.

COMPARE
Check the broad convex show face for the same transverse scar, then dry-fit
the band on the existing joint. The support-contact underside is the glue
face. Compare the exposed face and the edges separately. If the scar remains,
these precision changes have not resolved it; do not promote them as a fix.
