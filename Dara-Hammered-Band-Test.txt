SMOOTH COPPER COVER BAND - ONE JOINT TEST

Open Dara-Hammered-Band-Test.3mf AS A PROJECT in Bambu Studio.
Printer: Bambu Lab A1, 0.4 mm nozzle, textured PEI plate.
Map filament 1 to the copper silk spool used for the successful hammered test.
Keep the saved orientations and support painting. The three parts are tilted
55 degrees and already sliced with tree supports. No print job was sent.

Studio estimate: 21.27 g and 1 hour 30 minutes 14 seconds, supports included.

PARTS
T2: male half cropped from the redesigned full-size M2.
T3: female half cropped from the redesigned full-size M3.
B1: the complete, full-size band for that crown joint.
The body sample is 37 mm long along the joint normal, with the real stroke
width, edge curve, 10.5 mm body and hammered dome retained. This is one
joint pair, not a flow-calibration array. The body pieces use 15% gyroid.
The small band is solid in the slice because its thickness is only 1.2 mm.

WHAT CHANGED
The separate band bridges the central seam. It follows the smooth crown
and rests in a 13.2 mm-wide recessed seat. Its nominal width is 12 mm,
thickness 1.2 mm and edge chamfer 0.25 mm. The seat is 0.6 mm below the
smooth crown. The modeled assembly includes 0.15 mm for an adhesive layer;
the dry band can sit lower without that layer. The band ends sit 0.15 mm
inside the artwork outline. There are no clips, pins or rear projections.

The underlying lap uses the reference shoulder and key profiles, with
0.003 inch removed from the free shelf/key tips. The nominal face gap is
0.075 mm. This test does not depend on closing that hairline completely.
The previous close-seam test did not materially improve the physical fit.

CHECK AFTER PRINTING
1. Remove supports from the backs, lap and band underside. Clear loose
   support residue without reshaping the shoulder or sanding the show face.
2. Fit T2 and T3 together at their natural stop, with the backs on a flat
   surface. Leave them unglued while assessing the result.
3. Place B1 in the two seats. Match its shaped ends to the real outline;
   the two ends are not interchangeable. It should bridge the seam without
   needing to force the joint closed.
4. Check the front appearance, whether the band rocks, the match at both
   ends, the border around it and the quality of its supported underside.
   Judge the silk sheen in the lighting where the finished frame will hang.

The test decides whether this band finish and seating arrangement are worth
carrying to the full assembly. Do not print the full M yet if the band rocks,
leaves an objectionable border or loses the finish you want.
