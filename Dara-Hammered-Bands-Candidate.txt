The B labels in this separate project refer to copper bands. They are not
the wall mounting blocks B1-B4 in the original Dara.3mf project.

The crown pair has mirrored cut positions. The outer pair shares the same
height, moved to assembled Y=-65 mm to clear existing screws and locating
holes. The center cut is at Y=0. The letter's original outline is asymmetric,
so paired bands have different lengths and individually fitted curvature.

The M remains 599.88 x 358.74 mm. Its original body is 10.5 mm thick, with
the successful 9 mm-pitch hammered texture and up to 2.2 mm crown height.
The nominal fitted band top reaches at most Z=13.45 mm from the rear.
All 10 screw pilots and 12 locator features remain at their original global
positions with their original geometry. Their ownership by individual M
sections can change because the cuts moved. No changes to the knot-side
mounting geometry are required by this CAD. Ten D14 screw regions retain
solid modifiers.

BAND DESIGN AND ASSEMBLY
Each band is a separate smooth curved strip, 12 mm wide and 1.2 mm thick,
with a small edge chamfer. Its underside follows a recessed smooth seat.
Seat width is 13.2 mm; recess is 0.6 mm below the untextured crown.
The modeled assembly has 0.15 mm adhesive allowance below the band; actual
height depends on the adhesive layer. Ends sit 0.15 mm inside the outline.
The bands sit entirely on the front, leaving the rear mounting plane clear.

The bands cover the seam; the keyed laps underneath align the body sections.
The reference lap shoulder, key and socket profiles are carried to the new
cuts. Key spacing is fitted across each local stroke. Free shelf/key ends
retain the 0.003-inch shortening. The hidden nominal face gap is 0.075 mm.
This design does not rely on the unsuccessful attempt to close the visible
gap by reducing its nominal CAD clearance to 0.025 mm.

After the small test is accepted, dry-assemble the full M on a flat surface.
An order compatible with the new lap directions is M2, M4, M6, M1, M3, M5.
M1 lowers over the tongues of M2 and M4. M3 lowers over M2 and M6.
M5 lowers over M2. Bond the structural laps with the backs aligned, then
fit and bond the cover bands. Check the actual frame locators before the
adhesive cures. Do not use a band to pull a misaligned structural joint flat.

PRINTING
Printer: Bambu Lab A1, 0.4 mm nozzle, textured PEI plate.
Map filament 1 to the copper silk spool used for the successful tests.
All pieces retain a 55-degree face tilt and a common assembled layer direction.
Each body has its own in-plane rotation for bed clearance. Keep orientations,
scale, support painting and the saved process when re-slicing.
Four walls, 15% gyroid body infill, 0.2 mm layers, 220 C nozzle, 55 C bed,
0.98 filament flow, 7.5 mm3/s volumetric limit and no ironing.
The thin bands slice solid. Supports contact their undersides; support removal
and the quality of those contact surfaces are part of the small test.

The full project contains native slices with tree supports and interfaces.
The decorative faces and seat edges are support-blocked. Downward backs,
lap surfaces and band undersides have support enforcers. Clear support
residue from mounting entrances and laps without enlarging the features.

DIGITAL CHECKS AND PHYSICAL LIMITS
Six body meshes and five bands are each one watertight solid. Pairwise
intersection checks found no meaningful interferences in the assembled CAD.
All 22 mounting regions were compared with the original meshes. Native
sliced geometry, modifiers, support painting and process settings were
compared with the staged project. Actual extrusion bounds include bead
width and arc extrema. These checks establish a printable digital candidate,
not the physical fit, bonding performance or stability of a full-size print.
The small band test is pending. Tall body sections remain a larger physical
test than the coupon. Print one full-size part before committing all six.

The previous Dara.3mf and Dara-Hammered-M-Candidate.3mf are preserved.
Continue using Dara.3mf for the frame, backers and hardware. If this banded
candidate is accepted, its six M sections replace all previous M sections.
Do not mix M parts across projects; their cut locations differ.

REGENERATION
Requires Python with numpy, trimesh, scipy, shapely, manifold3d and matplotlib,
the committed original CAD/STLs, the close-seam test's filament/process setup,
and the installed Bambu Studio CLI. From the repository root:
  python scripts/prepare_hammered_bands.py geometry
  python scripts/prepare_hammered_bands.py stage --test
  python scripts/prepare_hammered_bands.py slice --test
  python scripts/prepare_hammered_bands.py publish --test
  python scripts/prepare_hammered_bands.py stage
  python scripts/prepare_hammered_bands.py slice
  python scripts/prepare_hammered_bands.py publish
Scratch meshes and stages are in .work/hammered-bands; review records and
previews are in working/review/hammered-bands. Run git lfs pull after cloning.
