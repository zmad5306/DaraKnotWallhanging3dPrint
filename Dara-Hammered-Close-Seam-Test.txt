CHANGES FROM YOUR SAVED, SUPPORTED JOINT TEST
1. The nominal visible face gap is reduced from 0.075 mm to 0.025 mm.
   Each visible edge extends 0.025 mm toward the other. This extension
   tapers to zero at body Z=6.7 mm, above the hidden joint features.
2. The free end of the bottom shelf is shortened by 0.003 inches
   (0.0762 mm). Its nominal local end changes from X=18 to X=17.9238.
3. Both keys are shortened by the same 0.003 inches on that same end.
   Their nominal local ends change from X=15.75 to X=15.6738.

The mating shoulder and its inside curve are unchanged. The socket,
key widths/heights/opposite ends, rear locator hole, and back faces are
unchanged. The physical test is still a 42 x 30 mm crop at full M scale.
The hammered texture and 55-degree orientation are retained.

Your saved support painting is retained on all remaining original faces.
The removed tip fragments necessarily remove their painted surface area.
New vertical cut faces need no added support painting. The plate has been
re-sliced with tree supports and support interfaces, not just checked for
an enabled support setting. The saved process and filament settings are
retained: four walls, 15% gyroid, 0.2 mm layers, 220 C nozzle, 55 C bed,
0.98 filament flow, 7.5 mm3/s volumetric limit, and no ironing.

COMPARE WITH THE PREVIOUS PAIR
After cooling and support removal, dry-fit without forcing or gluing.
Compare seating, face gap, and back alignment with the previous pair.
The end relief is intended to prevent those tips from stopping assembly
early. We have not established which surface stopped the previous pair;
this test does not promise that the inside corner is or is not involved.
The 0.025 mm gap is a CAD target, not a guaranteed printed clearance.
