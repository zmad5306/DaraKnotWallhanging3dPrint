WHAT THIS TESTS
Two pieces cropped from the actual M2-to-M3 keyed lap. Together they cover
42 x 30 mm of the real assembly, retaining its full 10.5 mm structural body.
M2 is the left object on the plate and has the protruding tongue plus a rear
locator hole. M3 is the right object and has the mating recess.
This is a joint/support/appearance check, not another flow calibration.

The hammered face uses the supplied generator's 9 mm strike spacing,
0.55 mm maximum dimple depth, 0.5 mm mesh sampling, and seed 73.
One continuous random field covers the assembled M before section clipping.
The original coupon's crown is adapted to the M's actual outline, rising
up to 2.2 mm above the original face. The crown does not restart at a seam.
Relief is clamped to zero at the outside edge so no original body material
is removed. Back faces, lap profiles, keys, locator and mounting dimensions
are not scaled or deliberately altered. Numerical geometry checks verify this.

PRINT SETTINGS
The exact saved 55-degree rotation and filament/process profile come from
hammered_coupon_clean.3mf supplied by Zach, not the earlier flat-top C profile.
0.2 mm layers; 220 C nozzle; 55 C textured bed; 0.98 filament flow;
7.5 mm3/s volumetric limit; no ironing. Four structural walls and 15% gyroid
replace the sample's two walls and grid infill. Tree auto supports retain
the successful saved threshold/critical-region/build-plate-only settings.
The generated supports are near the foot of this small crop, away from the
decorative face. A larger part requires its own support and stability review.

AFTER PRINTING
Let the plate cool, remove the brim/supports, and inspect the contact areas.
Dry-fit the tongue into its mating recess without forcing or gluing it.
Check that the halves seat fully, their backs align, and the hammered face
continues across the seam without a height step. Check the locator hole
against the existing mating dowel if available. Note any cleanup needed on
the lap, keys or rear bearing face; those observations decide whether this
orientation is suitable for the complete parts.

LARGER PARTS
A geometric search at 55 degrees found M2 + M5 together at approximately
227.5 x 227.5 x 229.4 mm, with a conservative 2.2 mm face allowance.
That is a candidate for reducing six M sections to five, not yet a verified
supported print. Rebuild a merged section without its internal lap/seam;
do not scale up this coupon or simply glue/union overlapping old lap meshes.
All remaining mounting locations and external joints must stay unchanged.
Support footprint, bed adhesion, height clearance, and consistent layer
direction across the assembled M still need checking on the full layout.

The production Dara.3mf and complete 31-plate project are unchanged.
