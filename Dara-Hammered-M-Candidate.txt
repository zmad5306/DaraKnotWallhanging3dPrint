SIZE, TEXTURE AND ORIENTATION
Assembled outline: 599.88 x 358.74 mm; original mounting locations retained.
Structural body: 10.5 mm. Highest hammered point: 12.7 mm from the back.
The surface uses the successful sample's 9 mm dimple pitch, 0.55 mm depth,
seed 73, and 0.5 mm mesh grid, extended in one coordinate system across the M.
The crown follows distance from the actual outline and rises no more than
2.2 mm. It blends over 17.5 mm from an edge. Narrow strokes are more rounded;
wide strokes have flatter centers with the same hammered dimples. This
avoids an ever-larger bulge as the M gets wider. It is not a fixed-radius
spherical dome. No decorative recess cuts below the original 10.5 mm face.

All parts tilt 55 degrees from the bed, with one common direction relative
to the assembled M. Each part is then turned on the bed to fit. Their layer
directions therefore agree in the assembly; exact layer phases can differ.
Keep the saved orientations, scale and support painting when re-slicing.
Do not lay the pieces flat or run auto-orient before printing.

The six original sections are retained. Combining sections into five while
keeping a common layer direction approached 250 mm height before allowing
additional setup margin. The chosen six-part layout stays below 209 mm
model height and gives the actual supports room on the 256 mm bed.

JOINT REVISION CARRIED THROUGH ALL FIVE JOINTS
Nominal visible face gap: 0.025 mm instead of 0.075 mm.
Each edge extends 0.025 mm toward the mate, tapering to zero at body Z=6.7.
The lower shelf's free end is shortened 0.003 inches (0.0762 mm):
local X=18 becomes X=17.9238.
Both key tips on that same end are shortened by the same amount:
local X=15.75 becomes X=15.6738.
The mating shoulder and its root curve, socket, key widths/heights and
opposite ends are retained. Texture is continuous across the section cuts.
The nominal gap is a CAD target, not a guaranteed physical seam width.

MOUNTS AND PRINT SETTINGS
All 10 rear screw pilots and 12 rear locator features retain their original
geometry. Ten D14 modifier cylinders keep the screw regions at 100% cubic
infill. The remainder uses four walls and 15% gyroid, with 0.2 mm layers.
The copper settings come from the successful tilted test: 220 C nozzle,
55 C bed, 0.98 filament flow, 7.5 mm3/s volumetric limit, no ironing.
These differ from the older flat-top copper settings in Dara.3mf.

Supports are explicitly painted onto downward-facing back, edge and joint
surfaces. The hammered face and hole interiors have blocker paint.
The actual sliced interfaces stay below the decorative interior. Supports
still contact side edges, and the native paths extend roughly 0.45-1.02 mm
into mounting-hole entrances. Clear those mouths and the lap contact faces
when removing supports; do not enlarge the holes or remove the shoulder.
There are no interfaces deep in the blind mounting holes in the path check.

CHECKS AND LIMITS
All six meshes are single watertight solids. Assembly checks cover all five
joints and potential part collisions. The original body is preserved apart
from the specified free-end trims and upper seam extensions. Hidden sections,
mount geometry and screw reinforcement were checked.
Bambu Studio 02.08.02.61 sliced every plate. The saved native project was
compared with the input geometry, modifiers, support paint and settings.
Extrusion bounds include bead widths and arc extrema. Sparse infill,
supports and support interfaces exist on every plate; only copper slot 1
extrudes; no ironing or inspection pause was added.
The only native slice-info warnings concern traditional timelapse.
The support image samples real exported toolpaths for legibility.

Physical fit of the revised joint is still pending the small test. Successful
slicing does not establish full-size print stability or finished fit. Once
the coupon passes, M2 is a useful first full-size part because it contains
three joint interfaces and a mounting region. Inspect its finish and support
removal before committing the rest of the spool. A tall, full-size part is
a larger physical test than the coupon, even with the same surface formula.

The existing Dara.3mf and 31-plate production copy remain unchanged. Continue
using them for the frame and other non-M components. Use this candidate
instead of the old flat M production plates 21-26. Plate 31 in the old
project is an earlier finish test, not an extra production M part.
Original M assembly order: M1, M6, M2, M4, M3, M5; print order is independent.

REPRODUCING THE CANDIDATE
Generator: scripts/prepare_hammered_m_project.py
Requires Python with numpy, trimesh, scipy, shapely, manifold3d, matplotlib.
Uses the committed original M STLs, CAD and close-seam test profile.
From the repository root:
  python scripts/prepare_hammered_m_project.py geometry
  python scripts/prepare_hammered_m_project.py stage
  python scripts/prepare_hammered_m_project.py assembly
  python scripts/prepare_hammered_m_project.py combine
  python scripts/prepare_hammered_m_project.py slice --parts 0
  python scripts/prepare_hammered_m_project.py publish
Native slicing requires the installed Windows Bambu Studio executable.
Scratch intermediates are in .work/hammered-m-production and are not versioned.
Audits and previews are in working/review/hammered-m-production.
Use git lfs pull after cloning to retrieve the saved 3MF/STL binaries.
