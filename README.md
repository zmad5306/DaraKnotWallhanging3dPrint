# Dara knot wall hanging

**Current project: [Dara.3mf](Dara.3mf)** — open **as a project** in Bambu Studio. The full artwork is optimized for the **Bambu Lab A2L, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers**, with **8% gyroid structural bodies**, four walls, five top/bottom layers, local solid screw reinforcement and solid veneers.

**Joint correction, October 19:** the user trimmed the three raised pads from the lower lap and confirmed that the joint fits with both white and brown front surfaces flush. The same **0.20 mm raised pads are removed from all 14 production knot joints**; A2L plates **1–10** are resliced. Mortises, keys, glue recesses, visible outlines, colors and **8% gyroid** settings are preserved. At that revision, plates **11–20** and the A1 companion were preserved; the later veneer support update below replaces only plates **15–17**. See the [pad-removal validation](working/bambu/Dara-Photo-Frame-A2L/joint-pad-removal-validation.json). The physical sample was switched to the A1; an updated full-size A2L joint has not yet been physically reported.

The fresh A2L layout has **10 knot sections / 14 knot joints**, including **six fully hidden behind the M**, two partly covered and six fully exposed. The M has **five flat cores and three flat-backed hammered veneers**, leaving just **two visible M seams in the broad fork** totaling 105.6 mm in front projection. All convex crown and hammer texture is in the veneers. Visible cosmetic edges have zero nominal gap, with fitting/glue clearance underneath; cover bands are omitted.

The complete project contains **20 natively sliced plates and 62 assembly pieces**. Every veneer uses the same complete rotation and has its face plane along bed Y. The veneers retain their fused necks and now use continuous braced wall carriers with thin bed bands; trees supplement them. Keep all saved rotations and use the coordinated A2L knot, M and mounting hardware together.

**M joint revision, October 18:** narrow arch crowns are continuous in both layers. The two face seams now cross the broad fork; five cores use four plates, with C4/C5 together on plate 14. Core seams have no aligned runs under face seams; one isolated crossing is about 46 degrees. The longer face seams total 105.6 mm. [See the seam overlay](working/review/a2l-m-joints/M-staggered-seam-layout.png).

**Veneer support update, October 21:** A2L plates **15–17** now contain the approved **r5 wall cradle** and fresh native slices. Each veneer has a continuous 1.2 mm inclined backing, a 1.0 mm rear wall, two 1.0 mm connecting walls and two **4 mm wide × 0.6 mm thick bed bands**. The obsolete outer guard, curved local rail, crossbars, center strip and starter feet are removed. The rear and connecting walls retain **35 pointed lightening openings** across the three plates. All three veneers and their complete supports total **901.70 g**, with about **59 h 51 min** combined slicer time. Original fused necks, veneer geometry, shared orientation and saved process settings are preserved. The other **17 plates** and all A1 files are unchanged. See the [promotion validation](working/bambu/Dara-Photo-Frame-A2L/veneer-wall-promotion-validation.json) and [preserved-plate proof](working/bambu/Dara-Photo-Frame-A2L/veneer-wall-unchanged-plates.json). This is the user-approved production file update; physical wall rigidity, adhesion, warping resistance and cleanup remain unqualified.

**Knot test, plate 3:** the sides stay 68 mm wide through the lap and its transition into the coupon ends. The receiving lap has three bed-printed feet plus outer skirts, seating on upward-printed floors at assembly Z0.8. Supported underside stock has 0.8 mm clearance and cannot set the joint height; the skirts close the outside walls without a visible groove. The receiver's hidden back is recessed 0.8 mm. Preserve its saved Z placement and finish direction. White and wood roof levels have been put on exact layer boundaries; native toolpaths confirm matching assembled white/blue height of 13.6 mm and wood crests of 10.0 mm on both halves. The locating-key fit is retained.

Remove supports from the bulk underside, key roofs, foot sides and behind the skirts. Remove brim/burrs around the feet and skirts without sanding down their bed-printed bearing faces. Clean the tongue pocket floors, seat the feet and outer skirts, then check the seam in grazing light and with a straightedge. Dry-fit in the existing white-face jig before adhesive.
